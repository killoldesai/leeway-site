Leeway Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE] · Contact: [SUPPORT_EMAIL]

Leeway is a habit tracker. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your habits live on your phone. You don't need an account to use Leeway.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, we store your habits in your own cloud account so they can sync and be backed up.
  • We don't sell your personal information and we don't read your habits.
  • You can delete your cloud data at any time from inside the app.

What we collect and why

1. Information that stays on your device (no account)

Your habits, check-ins, notes, reminder times and settings are saved on your phone. Reminders are scheduled by your phone; they do not go through a server.

2. If you sign in with Google (optional)

  • Account details: your email address and a unique account ID from Google Sign-In, used to create and secure your Leeway account.
  • Your synced data: your habits, check-ins, notes and a few settings (day start time, gentle mode, leeway days per month), so they are backed up and available on your other devices.
  • Purpose: to provide sync and backup. We use it for nothing else.

Your sync data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, with access rules that let only your own account read your own rows.

2b. If you sign in with Apple (optional)

We receive an account ID and, if you choose to share it, your email address (or Apple's private relay address). We use it only to create and secure your account. If you delete your account, we also revoke Leeway's access in your Apple ID settings.

3. Crash and error reports

If the app crashes, it may send a report to Sentry with the error, the place in the code where it happened, and the app and device version. We configure it not to send your name, email, IP address or the names of your habits.

4. Advertising (rewarded video)

Leeway can offer an optional video to unlock an extra habit slot. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Only if you choose to watch a video, AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and information about the ad, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In regions that require it, you will be asked for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

5. Purchases (Leeway Pro)

If you buy Leeway Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm your purchase and unlock Pro. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID. We never receive your payment details.

6. Approximate location

If you watch an optional ad video, Google AdMob may derive your approximate location (for example, your city or region) from your IP address to show relevant ads. We do not collect your precise location.

7. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or financial information.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers that run the app for us, under their own privacy terms: Google (sign-in and AdMob), Apple (sign-in and purchases), RevenueCat (purchase management), Supabase (sync and account storage) and Sentry (crash reports). We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for third-party marketing. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • On your device: you can erase habits in the app or uninstall it.
  • In the cloud: open Settings → Sync → Delete cloud data. This permanently deletes your Leeway account and all synced data from our servers. Data on your phone is not touched.
  • By email or web: see how to delete your account.
  • Backups and logs are removed on a rolling schedule after deletion.

Your choices and rights

You can use Leeway with no account, export your data to CSV or JSON for free at any time, turn sync off, and delete your data. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, and to object to certain processing. Contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL] to exercise these rights.

Children

Leeway is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules on our servers. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
